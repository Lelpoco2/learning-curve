# Sources

Put here the material your courses are based on: **textbooks, lecture notes, official
syllabi, exam programs** (PDF with a text layer, Markdown or text). Subfolders are fine,
e.g. `Sources/Analisi 1/`.

The tutor checks statements against these files **first**, before any online source,
and cites them with a link to the exact page (e.g. `[[Sources/Book.pdf#page=42|Book, p. 42]]`).
When your book and an online source disagree (notation, conventions, hypotheses), your
book wins and the difference is pointed out.

Scanned PDFs without a text layer cannot be searched: run OCR on them first.
